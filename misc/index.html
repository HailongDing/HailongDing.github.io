Interests

Programming

I still remember the first time I saw a computer when in high school. It was an Apple computer using cassette tape to record code. We made it play music by using BASIC language. I like programming.

Since then I have just kept following the technology around. I started with ASP, back when a web page was still put together on the server. Then Delphi, for client/server desktop applications. Then C and C++, much closer to the hardware: boot loaders, drivers, and the video processing that turns a camera into an intelligent analysis device.

The cloud era made Python the natural tool, mostly for scripts that collect diagnostics and pick apart logs and crash dumps. Now it is the AI era, and I use AI tools every day, at work and on side projects at home. Whatever comes after this one, I expect I will end up learning that too.

Photography

I like photography. When I raise the camera and everything else drops away, it feels like opening my eyes for the first time. The world is right there, and for once I am actually looking at it.

So I walk a lot. I take a camera through the streets and back alleys of the city and wait. Sooner or later something surfaces in a face: delight, sadness, concentration, or the blank look of somebody thinking about something else.

Those moments stay with me. Each picture is a fragment of a life that is not my own, and I have collected a lot of them by now. It makes my own life feel several times longer than it is.
